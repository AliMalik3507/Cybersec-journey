\# Blue Team Introduction

\*\*Path:\*\* SOC Level 1



\## Basic SOC workflow

1\. Alert comes in

2\. Analyst triages it

3\. Gather evidence

4\. Decide severity

5\. Respond or escalate

6\. Document everything



\## Security team structure

\- Blue team (defensive)

\- Red team (offensive)

\- GRC (governance, risk, compliance)



\## Humans as an attack vector

Two key ways to defend:

1\. \*\*Mitigation\*\* — prevent or reduce the chance \& impact of an attack

2\. \*\*Detection\*\* — detect and investigate activity that gets through



\## Supply chain attacks

A threat actor breaches an app or library, then pushes a malicious update to all its users — every user who installs it is compromised.
